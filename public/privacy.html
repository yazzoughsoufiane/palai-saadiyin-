Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Cosyrac ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data when you interact with our website at cosyrac.com or submit a request through our advertising forms.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a viewing request or contact form — including through Meta (Instagram/Facebook) lead generation forms — we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if provided)
  • Any message or details you voluntarily share

We do not collect payment information directly. Payments are processed through secure third-party providers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your viewing or purchase request
  • Schedule and conduct a private collection consultation (video call)
  • Send order confirmations and shipping updates
  • Improve our communication and service quality

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Meta Lead Ads

If you submit your information through a Meta (Facebook or Instagram) lead generation advertisement, your data is transmitted to us via Meta's secure platform. Meta's own Privacy Policy also applies to data collected through their platforms.

4. Data Retention

We retain your contact information only as long as necessary to fulfill your request or maintain our business relationship. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us directly.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

6. Cookies

Our website may use basic analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand traffic patterns. These tools may set cookies on your device. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.
